# Animal Farm 360 

It is a desktop-based farm management and e-commerce application designed to simplify livestock farm operations, product sales and administrative tasks.

The system provides separate role-based portals for buyers, employees and administrators. It allows customers to purchase live animals and organic farm products, schedule farm visits and submit feedback, while employees can access their work-related information and administrators can manage overall farm operations.

## Key Features

### 1. Public & Visitor Portal
- Browse available farm animals.
- Explore organic farm products.
- View animal details, including breed, health status, source and pricing.
- Reserve farm tours and educational visits by selecting dates, time slots and visitor counts.
- Submit reviews and ratings through the feedback system.
- Access About Us and Contact Us sections.

### 2. Buyer / Customer Portal
- User registration and secure login.
- Browse and purchase live animals and farm products.
- Add items to the shopping cart.
- Provide contact and delivery information.
- Place and manage orders.

### 3. Employee Portal
- View personal employee information, designation, department and joining date.
- Check daily attendance records and work status.
- View monthly payroll and salary information.
- Track payment status and salary disbursement history.

### 4. Admin & Management Dashboard
- Add, edit and manage employee records.
- Track employee attendance and manage salaries.
- Add, update and organize animal records and agricultural products.
- Review, confirm and cancel customer orders.
- Manage incoming farm tour bookings.
- Approve and verify farm visit requests.
